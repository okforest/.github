**Grow what your AI knows.**

OKForest turns an organization's institutional knowledge into a governed,
AI-ready asset. Teams keep knowledge as
[Open Knowledge Format (OKF)](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format)
bundles in their own Git repositories. OKForest serves that knowledge to AI
assistants over MCP and to people in a web browser, routes every change
through owner and steward approval as a pull request, and federates many
repositories, private and public, into one discoverable whole. You own the
content: cancel, and your knowledge stays in your repos with its full history.
